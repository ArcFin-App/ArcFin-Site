Contribute to Arcfin

Help shape a thoughtful native Jellyfin experience for the Apple ecosystem.

Arcfin is not open source yet

The project will become open source, but it is currently closed source while the app, documentation, and contribution process are being put in order. That preparation matters, and the goal is to open the project with a clear foundation for everyone who joins.

Ways to help today

  • Try the TestFlight build and share clear, reproducible feedback.
  • Suggest improvements to the experience, accessibility, or platform support.
  • Report bugs with your device, operating system version, server details, and steps to reproduce.
  • Share ideas for integrations with Jellyfin, Seerr, Streamystats, and related tools.

Get in touch

For contribution ideas, early collaboration, or questions about the future open-source project, email:

contribution.arcfin@psmatas.com

Before you email

Please avoid sending passwords, server tokens, private media details, or other sensitive information. For security vulnerabilities, use the private security contact on the Support page instead.
