Privacy Policy

What happens to your data when you use Arcfin.

Effective 23 November 2026

On this page

In short: Arcfin has no server of its own. It connects directly from your device to the servers you add. We don’t collect, see or store your media, your passwords or what you watch. There’s no account with us, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking. This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies.

Arcfin (“the app”) is developed by Puranjay Savar Mattas (“we”, “us”). This policy applies to Arcfin on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

What Arcfin connects to

Arcfin is a client for services you run or already use. When you add a server or turn on a feature, your device talks to it directly:

  • Your Jellyfin server (required). Arcfin sends your sign-in details and playback requests straight to the address you enter. If you install the Arcfin Sync plugin on that server, your Arcfin settings are stored there too.
  • Seerr (optional). If you connect a Seerr server to browse and request titles, your device talks to it directly.
  • TMDB (optional). If you add your own TMDB API key, Arcfin asks The Movie Database for review scores, where-to-watch information, popular titles and artwork, using the IDs of the titles you look at.
  • Wikidata. To show awards on a title page, Arcfin sends that title’s TMDB ID to Wikidata’s public query service.
  • YouTube. If your server has no trailer for a title, Arcfin can play its trailer from YouTube using Google’s embedded player. You can turn this off in Settings › Playback.
  • ListenBrainz (optional, per profile). It’s off until you add your own ListenBrainz user token in Settings › Integrations. With a token, Arcfin sends your listens to ListenBrainz, each with the song title, artist, album, its length, when you played it, and that it came from Arcfin and which Arcfin version. It also sends the loves and hates you set on a song, reads your feedback and your listening stats for Stats and Year in Music, and checks that the token is valid. If you import your listening history while ListenBrainz is linked, the plays from the files you pick are sent to ListenBrainz with their times, as well as to your Jellyfin server. There’s a switch for this when you import.
  • What’s Playing? (iPhone and iPad). To identify a song playing nearby, Arcfin uses Apple’s ShazamKit and the microphone, only while you’re on that screen. ShazamKit makes a signature of the sound on your device and matches it with Apple’s Shazam service. Arcfin doesn’t keep the recording or send it anywhere else. When the song isn’t on your server and you’ve turned on the Apple Music option in Settings, Arcfin searches Apple Music’s catalogue with MusicKit to find and play it.
  • Voice search in CarPlay. Arcfin uses the microphone and Apple’s speech recognition to turn what you say into a search. Apple may process the audio under its own privacy policy. Arcfin only uses the resulting text, to search your server.

We operate none of these. We have no view of your library, your watch history, your requests or your settings.

What’s stored, and where

DataWhere it lives
Server addresses, sign-in tokens, Seerr sign-ins, your TMDB key and your ListenBrainz tokenYour device’s Keychain, encrypted by the system. Only ever sent to the service it belongs to.
Profile lock (PIN, Face ID, Touch ID)Your device only. Face ID and Touch ID data never leave Apple’s Secure Enclave; Arcfin only receives a yes or no.
Downloaded videos, subtitles and artworkYour device’s storage, for offline playback. Removed when you delete the download or the app.
Playback progress, caches and preferencesYour device’s app storage. Progress is also saved to your Jellyfin server, as with any Jellyfin app.
Settings, if you use the Arcfin Sync pluginYour Jellyfin server, so they follow you to your other devices.
StatsWorked out on your device from the watch history your Jellyfin server already keeps. If you’ve linked ListenBrainz, music stats and Year in Music can come from your ListenBrainz stats instead.
Wi-Fi network nameRead on your device to switch between your server’s home and remote addresses. Only the name you mark as “home” is kept, and it’s never sent anywhere.
The app’s diagnostic logYour device only, until you choose Share Diagnostics. It can include your server’s address and the titles you played, so check it before posting it publicly.
Siri, Spotlight, widgets and HandoffTitles you watch can appear in Spotlight, widgets and Siri suggestions. Apple handles these on your device under its own privacy terms.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t run a server your data passes through.
  • The app uses no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services of any kind.
  • We don’t ask for your name, email address or an account to use the app.
  • We don’t sell or share data, because we don’t collect any.

Other services

  • Google Cast (Chromecast, iPhone and iPad only) uses Google’s Cast SDK to find and control Cast devices on your network, under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube trailers are subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • TMDB requests are subject to TMDB’s Privacy Policy. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • ListenBrainz is run by the MetaBrainz Foundation, under the MetaBrainz Foundation’s Privacy Policy.
  • Wikidata queries are subject to the Wikimedia Foundation’s Privacy Policy.
  • Apple services such as AirPlay, SharePlay, Picture in Picture, Siri, notifications, ShazamKit and Shazam, MusicKit and Apple Music, speech recognition and TestFlight are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

We don’t control these services and aren’t responsible for how they handle data.

Permissions

  • Local Network, to find your Jellyfin server and Cast devices on your network, and for Set Up Nearby Device, which signs in an Apple TV, iPad or Mac from your iPhone or iPad on the same network. Several devices can be waiting; you set them up one at a time, and you can send several profiles in one go.
    • For each profile you choose, your iPhone or iPad sends your Jellyfin user name, the server’s name and its addresses and, if you added them, your TMDB key, the profile’s Seerr server address, sign-in method and Seerr user name, and your ListenBrainz token. It never sends a password: Jellyfin signs in on the new device through Quick Connect, and a local Seerr account signs in on the new device itself.
    • The new device sends back a Quick Connect code for each profile. Only after you’ve pressed Allow on it does it also send which Jellyfin accounts are already signed in there, so your iPhone or iPad doesn’t offer them again.
    • Both screens show the same six-digit code to check first. Nothing is sent until you’ve pressed Allow on the new device, and your profiles aren’t sent until you’ve also confirmed the code on your iPhone or iPad. Everything after that is encrypted and goes directly between your devices over your local network, never to us.
  • Bluetooth (iPhone and iPad), which Google Cast can use to find nearby Cast devices.
  • Location, only to read the current Wi-Fi name for home and remote switching. Your location is never stored, tracked or shared.
  • Face ID, only to unlock a profile you’ve locked.
  • Camera (iPhone and iPad), only while you scan the Quick Connect code an Apple TV shows to sign it in. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Microphone (iPhone and iPad), only while you’re on the What’s Playing? screen, to identify a song playing nearby, and for voice search in CarPlay.
  • Speech Recognition (iPhone), to turn what you say to voice search in CarPlay into a search.
  • Media & Apple Music (iPhone and iPad), only if you turn on the Apple Music option, to find and play a song What’s Playing? identified that isn’t on your server.
  • Notifications, to tell you about new episodes and downloads. Arcfin checks your own server for new episodes.

Each one is optional. If you say no, only the feature that needs it stops working.

This website

arcfin.psmattas.com is a static site with no advertising and no tracking across other sites. Apart from Cloudflare’s own scripts described below, it loads no fonts, scripts or images from other services.

  • Cloudflare delivers the site and protects it from abuse. To do that it processes your IP address and the details of each request, and it may set a security cookie such as cf_clearance or __cf_bm when it checks that a visitor isn’t an automated attack. These cookies are strictly necessary for the site to stay available and aren’t used to track you. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy and its cookie list.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to the site. A small script from Cloudflare reports the page you opened, the page that linked you there, your browser and device type, your country (worked out from your IP address, which isn’t stored) and how quickly the page loaded. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage and doesn’t follow you to other sites. We use these numbers to spot abuse and attacks, to see which pages people read, and to decide which languages to add next. See what Cloudflare Web Analytics collects.
  • Your choices, in your browser. The site remembers your appearance (System, Light or Dark), your language and that you’ve seen the cookie notice, in your browser’s local storage under arcfin-theme, arcfin-lang and arcfin-cookies. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser’s data for the site removes them.

Because these are strictly necessary, only store a choice you made, or count visits without cookies or identifying you, the site doesn’t ask for consent. The notice at the bottom of the page is there so you know about them. To opt out of the visit counting, block static.cloudflareinsights.com with a content blocker; the site works the same without it.

Children

Arcfin isn’t directed at children. Content restrictions and parental controls are set on your Jellyfin server, and Arcfin follows them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything Arcfin stored on that device: downloads, caches, preferences and Keychain entries. Your library, accounts and watch history live on your Jellyfin server and aren’t affected; manage them there. Settings saved by the Arcfin Sync plugin can be removed from the plugin on your server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. The source code is public, so you can check what the app does yourself.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support.arcfin@psmattas.com. See also Data Protection.
