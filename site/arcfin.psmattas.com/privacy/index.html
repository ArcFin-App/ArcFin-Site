Privacy Policy

What happens to your data when you use Arcfin.

Effective 24 November 2026

On this page

In short: Arcfin has no server of its own. It connects directly from your device to the servers you add. We don’t collect, see or store your media, your passwords or what you watch. There’s no account with us, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking. This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies.

Arcfin (“the app”) is developed by Puranjay Savar Mattas (“we”, “us”). This policy applies to Arcfin on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch, and in CarPlay.

What Arcfin connects to

Arcfin is a client for services you run or already use. When you add a server or turn on a feature, your device talks to it directly:

  • Your Jellyfin server (required). Arcfin sends your sign-in details and playback requests straight to the address you enter.
  • Arcfin Sync (optional, a plugin on your Jellyfin server). If your server’s admin installs it, your synced settings are stored on that server, with a record of each device you use Arcfin on: its name, platform, app version, when it was last seen and how it syncs. The server’s admins can set defaults and locks for settings. From version 0.6 the admin can also turn on watch parties, Seerr and TMDB through the server, signed security commands, watch time limits, download limits, shared accounts, announcements, hidden titles and recommendations; each is off until they do. What these keep is listed below. All of this stays on your server; we never receive it.
  • Seerr (optional). If you connect a Seerr server, your device talks to it directly for requests and your watchlist, signed in per profile.
  • TMDB (optional). If you add your own TMDB API key, Arcfin asks The Movie Database for review scores, where-to-watch information, popular titles and images, using the IDs of the titles you look at.
  • Wikidata. To show awards on a title page, Arcfin sends that title’s TMDB ID to Wikidata’s public query service.
  • LRCLIB (optional). If you turn on Find Missing Lyrics in Settings › Music, Arcfin looks up lyrics your server doesn’t have on LRCLIB, a free lyrics library, sending it the song’s title, artist, album and length.
  • YouTube (iPhone, iPad and Mac). If your server has no trailer for a title and YouTube Fallback is on in Settings › Playback, Arcfin plays its trailer from YouTube using Google’s embedded player.
  • Google Cast (Chromecast, iPhone and iPad). When you cast, Google’s Cast SDK finds Cast devices on your local network, and the one you pick plays from your server.
  • ListenBrainz (optional, per profile). It’s off until you add your own ListenBrainz user token in Settings › Integrations. With a token, Arcfin sends your listens to ListenBrainz, each with the song title, artist, album, its length, when you played it, and that it came from Arcfin and which Arcfin version. It also sends the loves and hates you set on a song, reads your feedback and your listening stats for Stats and Year in Music, and checks that the token is valid. If you import your listening history while ListenBrainz is linked, the plays from the files you pick are sent to ListenBrainz with their times, as well as to your Jellyfin server. The switch for this is on by default; turn it off when you import to keep those plays off ListenBrainz.
  • What’s Playing? (optional, iPhone and iPad). It’s off until you turn it on in Settings › Music. To identify a song playing nearby, Arcfin uses Apple’s ShazamKit and the microphone, only while you’re on that screen. ShazamKit makes a signature of the sound on your device and matches it with Apple’s Shazam service. Arcfin doesn’t keep the recording or send it anywhere else. It then looks for the song on your server, and can open it in Apple Music.
  • Apple Music (optional, iPhone and iPad). If you have an Apple Music subscription and turn on Apple Music in Settings › Music, music search also looks in Apple Music’s catalogue with MusicKit, sending Apple your search, and plays songs that aren’t on your server in Apple Music’s own player.
  • Voice search in CarPlay (iPhone). Arcfin uses the microphone and Apple’s speech recognition to turn what you say into a search. Apple may process the audio under its own privacy policy. Arcfin only uses the resulting text, to search your server.
  • Apple Watch. Your iPhone sends the watch your active profile’s server addresses and sign-in over Apple’s WatchConnectivity. The watch keeps the sign-in in its own Keychain and then talks to your Jellyfin server itself. Signing out on your iPhone clears it.

We operate none of these. We have no view of your library, your watch history, your requests or your settings.

What’s stored, and where

DataWhere it lives
Server addresses, sign-in tokens, Seerr sign-ins, your TMDB key and your ListenBrainz tokenYour device’s Keychain, encrypted by the system. Only ever sent to the service it belongs to. If your server’s admin sets up Seerr or TMDB in the Arcfin Sync plugin, Arcfin uses them through your server instead and removes the Seerr password and TMDB key from your device.
Profile lock (PIN, Face ID, Touch ID)Your device only. Face ID and Touch ID data never leave Apple’s Secure Enclave; Arcfin only receives a yes or no. Your PIN is never sent anywhere. If your server uses the Arcfin Sync plugin’s security features, your server’s admin can send signed commands that clear a profile’s PIN, lock or unlock it, or wipe a lost device’s downloads, and Arcfin tells the server when a profile locks after too many wrong PINs.
The sign-in on your Apple WatchThe watch’s Keychain, sent from your iPhone. Cleared when you sign out on your iPhone.
Downloaded videos, music, subtitles and artworkYour device’s storage, for offline playback, and music you keep on your Apple Watch. Removed when you delete the download or the app.
Playback progress, caches and preferencesYour device’s app storage. Progress is also saved to your Jellyfin server, as with any Jellyfin app.
Settings and the record of your devices, if your server has the Arcfin Sync pluginYour Jellyfin server, so your settings follow you to your other devices. You can rename or forget your devices in Settings › Server › Devices & Sync.
Arcfin Sync 0.6 features, if your server’s admin turns them onYour Jellyfin server, for the feature to work:
  • Security: each Arcfin device’s ID, name and which version of the server’s signing key it holds, which devices you’ve trusted, approval requests for new devices, and reports that a device saw a key that didn’t match.
  • Watch time: your daily limits and bedtime, and how many minutes you’ve watched each day.
  • Download limits: which titles and songs each of your devices keeps downloaded, and their sizes, to count them once across your devices.
  • Watch parties: each party’s host, guests, title, time and the time zone each guest’s device reports, until it’s over.
  • History: past watch parties and Pick Together sessions, with who took part and what was picked, only if the admin keeps history, and for as long as they choose.
  • Seerr through the server: requests made while Seerr can’t be reached, until they’re sent (at most three days). The admin’s Seerr and TMDB keys are kept encrypted on the server and never sent to your device.
  • Other features: titles you hide from Continue Watching and Next Up; on a shared account, who you said is watching, for twelve hours; announcements and maintenance the admin posts. Recommendations are worked out on the server from the watch history it already keeps, and Popular on This Server only shows titles at least two other people played.
StatsWorked out on your device from the watch history your Jellyfin server already keeps. If you’ve linked ListenBrainz, music stats and Year in Music can come from your ListenBrainz stats instead.
Wi-Fi network nameRead on your device to switch between your server’s home and remote addresses. Only the name you mark as “home” is kept, and it’s never sent anywhere.
The app’s diagnostic logYour device only. Share Diagnostics builds it on your device and opens the system share sheet, so you choose where it goes; nothing is sent automatically. It can include your server’s address and the titles you played, so check it before posting it publicly. Report a Problem only opens our public issue tracker in your browser.
Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, widgets, Live Activities and HandoffTitles you watch or play can appear in Spotlight, widgets, Live Activities and Siri suggestions. Apple handles these on your device under its own privacy terms.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t run a server your data passes through.
  • The app uses no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services of any kind.
  • We don’t ask for your name, email address or an account to use the app.
  • We don’t sell or share data, because we don’t collect any.

Other services

  • Google Cast (Chromecast, iPhone and iPad only) uses Google’s Cast SDK to find and control Cast devices on your network, under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube trailers are subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • TMDB requests are subject to TMDB’s Privacy Policy. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • ListenBrainz is run by the MetaBrainz Foundation, under the MetaBrainz Foundation’s Privacy Policy.
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net) is a free, open lyrics library, run independently of us under its own terms.
  • Wikidata queries are subject to the Wikimedia Foundation’s Privacy Policy.
  • Apple services are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy: AirPlay, SharePlay for watching or listening together over FaceTime, Picture in Picture, Siri and Shortcuts, Spotlight, widgets, Live Activities, Handoff, notifications, CarPlay, the Apple Watch and WatchConnectivity, ShazamKit and Shazam, MusicKit and Apple Music, speech recognition, and TestFlight, including nightly builds for internal testers.

We don’t control these services and aren’t responsible for how they handle data.

Permissions

  • Local Network, to find your Jellyfin server and Cast devices on your network, and for Set Up Nearby Device, which signs in an Apple TV, iPad or Mac from your iPhone or iPad on the same network. Several devices can be waiting; you set them up one at a time, and you can send several profiles in one go.
    • For each profile you choose, your iPhone or iPad sends your Jellyfin user name, the server’s name and its addresses and, if you added them, your TMDB key, the profile’s Seerr server address, sign-in method and Seerr user name, and your ListenBrainz token. It never sends a password: Jellyfin signs in on the new device through Quick Connect, and a local Seerr account signs in on the new device itself.
    • The new device sends back a Quick Connect code for each profile. Only after you’ve pressed Allow on it does it also send which Jellyfin accounts are already signed in there, so your iPhone or iPad doesn’t offer them again.
    • Both screens show the same six-digit code to check first. Nothing is sent until you’ve pressed Allow on the new device, and your profiles aren’t sent until you’ve also confirmed the code on your iPhone or iPad. Everything after that is encrypted and goes directly between your devices over your local network, never to us.
  • Bluetooth (iPhone and iPad), which Google Cast can use to find nearby Cast devices.
  • Location, only to read the current Wi-Fi name for home and remote switching. Your location is never stored, tracked or shared.
  • Face ID, only to unlock a profile you’ve locked.
  • Camera (iPhone and iPad), only while you scan a code: the Quick Connect code an Apple TV shows to sign it in, or a Pick Together or watch party code. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Microphone (iPhone and iPad), only while you’re on the What’s Playing? screen, to identify a song playing nearby, and for voice search in CarPlay.
  • Speech Recognition (iPhone), to turn what you say to voice search in CarPlay into a search.
  • Media & Apple Music (iPhone and iPad), only if you turn on Apple Music in Settings › Music, to find and play songs that aren’t on your server.
  • Siri (iPhone and iPad), so Siri can play your movies, shows and music in Arcfin when you ask, including in the car.
  • Notifications, to tell you about new episodes and downloads. Arcfin checks your own server for new episodes.

Each one is optional. If you say no, only the feature that needs it stops working.

This website

arcfin.psmattas.com is a static site with no advertising and no tracking across other sites. Apart from Cloudflare’s own scripts described below, it loads no fonts, scripts or images from other services.

  • Cloudflare delivers the site and protects it from abuse. To do that it processes your IP address and the details of each request, and it may set a security cookie such as cf_clearance or __cf_bm when it checks that a visitor isn’t an automated attack. These cookies are strictly necessary for the site to stay available and aren’t used to track you. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy and its cookie list.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to the site. A small script from Cloudflare reports the page you opened, the page that linked you there, your browser and device type, your country (worked out from your IP address, which isn’t stored) and how quickly the page loaded. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage and doesn’t follow you to other sites. We use these numbers to spot abuse and attacks, to see which pages people read, and to decide which languages to add next. See what Cloudflare Web Analytics collects.
  • Your choices, in your browser. The site remembers your appearance (System, Light or Dark), your language and that you’ve seen the cookie notice, in your browser’s local storage under arcfin-theme, arcfin-lang and arcfin-cookies. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser’s data for the site removes them.

Because these are strictly necessary, only store a choice you made, or count visits without cookies or identifying you, the site doesn’t ask for consent. The notice at the bottom of the page is there so you know about them. To opt out of the visit counting, block static.cloudflareinsights.com with a content blocker; the site works the same without it.

Children

Arcfin isn’t directed at children. Content restrictions and parental controls are set on your Jellyfin server, and Arcfin follows them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything Arcfin stored on that device: downloads, caches, preferences and Keychain entries. Your library, accounts and watch history live on your Jellyfin server and aren’t affected; manage them there. Settings, device records and everything else the Arcfin Sync plugin keeps can be removed by your server’s admin from the plugin on your server. Listens and feedback you sent to ListenBrainz are managed in your ListenBrainz account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. The source code is public, so you can check what the app does yourself.
