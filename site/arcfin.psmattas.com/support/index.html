Connecting your server

Arcfin connects to a Jellyfin server you already run — it isn't a hosted service. If your server isn't reachable, check that Arcfin has Local Network permission in iOS Settings (for discovery on your home network) and that your server's address is reachable from your device. For anything Jellyfin-specific, the Jellyfin documentation is the best resource.

Security

Found a security vulnerability? Please don't post it publicly — report it privately so it can be fixed first.

Email

security@psmattas.com

Include a description, steps to reproduce, and potential impact. I acknowledge reports within 48 hours and aim to ship a fix within 14 days depending on severity.

Data & privacy questions

Arcfin has no backend and doesn't collect analytics — see the Privacy Policy and Data Protection pages for the full detail. For anything not answered there, use the general support email above.
