Server plugin

Arcfin Sync

An optional plugin for your Jellyfin server. Arcfin works fine without it; with it, your settings follow you between devices, admins can set defaults and locks for everyone, households get watch parties and safety controls, and downloads get smaller.

Latest version 0.3.0 · Free and open source under the AGPL-3.0

On this page

What it adds

Newest first. Every feature from version 0.6.0 is off until the server’s admin turns it on, and anything already in use when the plugin updates stays on. Arcfin works fine without the plugin.

  • Watch parties. Watch something together, each on your own screen. Start a party on iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple TV, from Home or a title’s page: pick a movie, or a show with its season and episode. Guests join on any of them with a six-character code, or on iPhone and iPad by scanning a QR code, never a link. You can plan a party up to 30 days ahead, and as host you see what time it’ll be for each guest where they are. Arcfin hosts a Jellyfin SyncPlay group, so people in other apps that support SyncPlay can join too; the host needs Arcfin. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Seerr and TMDB through your server. The admin puts the Seerr API key and the TMDB key in the plugin. They’re stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM and never reach any device. Every Seerr call is made as the person using it, with their own permissions and request limits, and only to the parts of Seerr Arcfin needs. Requests made while Seerr is down wait in a queue and are sent when it’s back. Seerr can have a local address for the server and a public one for the apps. Without this, each device signs in to Seerr itself, as before. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Commands only your server can give. The server has its own signing key, which it can change on a schedule, with each new key signed by the last one. Arcfin carries out a command only when that key has signed it: clear a forgotten PIN, lock or unlock a profile on all of someone’s devices (and, if you choose, disable their Jellyfin account too), or wipe a lost device, which deletes its downloads, locks it and signs it out. PINs never leave the device. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Trusting the server’s key. Over HTTPS, devices trust the key on their own. Over plain HTTP, someone confirms the key’s fingerprint by typing its last four characters, or Arcfin checks it against the server’s saved HTTPS address. For a household on its own network, admins can choose to trust the key over HTTP. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • PIN rules and trusted devices. Admins choose who needs a PIN (nobody, everyone or chosen people), the shortest PIN allowed (4 to 8 digits) and how many wrong tries lock a profile. With trusted devices on, a new device shows a code and waits until one of that person’s trusted iPhones or iPads, or an admin, approves it. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Safer sign-ins. Admins can require HTTPS away from home; local network addresses don’t need it. They choose what happens to a device that falls behind on key changes (sign it out, or sign it out and lock it), and can sign out older versions of Arcfin that can’t follow these rules. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Watch time and bedtime. Give each person daily minutes, for weekdays and weekends, and a bedtime, in their own time zone. Time is counted across every app on the server. Arcfin warns them 10 and 5 minutes before, then stops playback with a message. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Download limits. Cap how many titles and how many GB people can keep downloaded for movies and shows, and how many albums, songs and GB for music, whichever comes first, with your own limits for any one person. A title on several of someone’s devices counts once. Downloads and Downloaded Music show how much is used. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Who’s Watching? On an account several people share, like the one on the living-room TV, Arcfin asks who’s watching and copies progress and watched status to that person too. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Announcements and planned maintenance. Messages from the admin, and times when the server will be down for maintenance, show at the top of Home. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Hide from Continue Watching and Next Up. Hide a title or a show and it’s hidden on all your devices, kept for you on the server. It comes back when you play it again. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Recommendations. Top Picks, Because You Watched and Popular on This Server rows on Home. Popular only shows titles at least two other people played in the last 30 days. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Live sessions and history. Admins can end, lock, remove people from or reschedule any watch party or Pick Together session, and choose how long their history is kept: a number of days, or for ever. Version 0.6.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Pick Together. Can’t decide what to watch? Everyone swipes through titles on their own iPhone or iPad until you all like the same one, then watches it on one device, on another, or together in sync with SyncPlay. People join by scanning a QR code or entering a code, and each person only gets titles everyone in the session is allowed to see. The device that starts a session hosts it: it deals new titles, removes people and locks the session. Version 0.5.0 and later, with Arcfin 0.5.0 (build 3) or later.
  • Settings that follow you. Change a setting on your iPhone and it’s there on your iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Settings that only make sense on one kind of device, like Mac window options or Apple TV focus options, stay on that kind of device.
  • Devices & Sync. Each device chooses how it syncs: automatically, Ask First to check with you before using changes from another device, or Off. You can also send one device’s settings to all your others. It’s in Arcfin under Settings › Server › Devices & Sync.
  • Your devices in one place. The plugin keeps a record of each device you use Arcfin on, with its name, platform, app version and when it was last seen. You can rename or forget your own devices, and admins can do it for anyone.
  • Defaults and locks for everyone. Admins give any setting a default, used until someone picks their own, or lock it so everyone gets that value and can’t change it. They can do it for everyone on the server or for one person, and unlock a setting for one person. In the app it’s Admin › Arcfin for Everyone; on Apple TV it’s in Settings › Server.
  • The full list of settings. The plugin carries the same catalogue of settings as the app, with their titles, defaults, choices and the platforms that show them. It only stores settings on that list.
  • Smaller downloads. The plugin tests which video encoders your server’s graphics card can really use. When it can encode AV1, Arcfin converts lower-quality downloads to AV1, which is about half the size of H.264 for the same picture. Admins can see the results in the app under Admin › Hardware Encoders.
  • Every audio track in a converted download. Jellyfin keeps only one audio track when it converts a download. The plugin hands Arcfin each extra track you pick, so a smaller download can keep several languages.

Installing it

You need to be an administrator on a Jellyfin server running version 12.1 or later. The steps are the same in any browser.

  1. Add the plugin repository. In Jellyfin, open Dashboard › Plugins › Repositories and add a new repository. Call it Arcfin, and paste this URL:
    https://git.psmattas.com/ArcFin/Arcfin-Sync/media/branch/main/manifest.json
  2. Restart Jellyfin. Use Restart on the dashboard, or restart the server or its container. Jellyfin reads the new repository when it starts.
  3. Install the plugin. Open Dashboard › Plugins › Catalog, find Arcfin Sync and choose Install.
  4. Restart Jellyfin again. Plugins load when the server starts. Afterwards, Arcfin Sync shows as Active under Dashboard › Plugins › My Plugins.
  5. Set it up. Arcfin Sync now has its own page in the dashboard (see below). Then open Arcfin and go to Settings › Server: under Arcfin Sync it shows Connected with the plugin’s version.

Updates come through the same repository. When a new version is out, Jellyfin offers it under My Plugins; install it and restart.

Setting it up

Open Arcfin Sync from the dashboard’s menu, or from My Plugins. It’s one page with nine tabs:

  • Overview: how the plugin works, with links to help and to report a problem.
  • Settings: every Arcfin setting. Give any of them a default, used until someone picks their own, or lock it so everyone gets your value.
  • Users & Devices: every device signed in with Arcfin. Rename or forget one, and give a single user their own defaults and locks.
  • Features: turn each feature on or off, like Pick Together, watch parties, recommendations and hiding from Continue Watching, and choose who can use Pick Together and watch parties.
  • Seerr & TMDB: the keys the server keeps for everyone, Seerr’s local and public addresses, and requests waiting to be sent.
  • Security: the server’s signing key and how devices trust it, PIN rules, trusted devices and HTTPS away from home.
  • Watch Time: daily minutes and bedtimes.
  • Household: shared accounts that ask Who’s Watching?, announcements and planned maintenance.
  • Downloads: how much each person can keep downloaded.

Admins can make the same changes in Arcfin, under Admin › Arcfin for Everyone. Changes reach each device the next time Arcfin syncs: when it opens, comes to the front, or a setting changes.

Users don’t need to do anything. Arcfin finds the plugin on its own, and Sync Now in Settings › Server syncs straight away. To choose how a device syncs, open Settings › Server › Devices & Sync.

Privacy

The plugin runs on your own Jellyfin server. Synced settings, the record of your devices, watch time, download limits and the Seerr and TMDB keys are stored there and nowhere else; there’s still no Arcfin server in the middle. The keys are encrypted, and PINs never leave your devices. Pick Together sessions end after six hours without activity. A history of past sessions and watch parties is kept only if the admin chooses, for as long as they choose. The Privacy Policy covers what’s kept.

What’s changed

Every release of the plugin is in the changelog, under Plugin. It updates on its own when a new version is published.

Something wrong with the plugin? Open an issue in the app repository and mention Arcfin Sync and its version.
