Roadmap

From first commit to the App Store.

On this page

What’s next

  1. 0.5.1

    The last beta, then the App Store

    No new features: stability fixes, and tests that cover everything Arcfin does. Once testers are happy with it, 0.5.1 goes to Apple for review and becomes the first App Store release.

  2. After

    A rolling cycle

    Nightly builds go to internal testers every night something has changed. A nightly they approve goes on to the public TestFlight beta, on the same link, and to the App Store once both groups have used it without problems. Security fixes skip the wait.

Plans can change. The milestones show what’s in each version as it happens.

Now

Version 0.2.0 on TestFlight

0.5.0, the last big batch of features. Music with Now Playing and lyrics, offline and smart downloads, CarPlay, SharePlay and widgets. Live TV with a guide, recordings and radio. An Admin tab with Arcfin for Everyone, Devices & Sync, Play On and remote control, and an Apple Watch app for your music and as a remote.

Build 3 adds Pick Together. With version 0.6.0 of the Arcfin Sync plugin it also brings watch parties, Seerr and TMDB through your server, commands to clear a PIN, lock a profile or wipe a lost device, watch time and bedtime, download limits, Who’s Watching? on shared accounts, announcements on Home and new recommendations. Each one is off until the server’s admin turns it on.

November 2026

  1. 23 November 2026

    0.5.0: music and remotes

    Build 1 arrives on TestFlight for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, with music and offline downloads, CarPlay, Live TV, an Admin tab with Arcfin for Everyone, Devices & Sync, Play On and an Apple Watch app.

  2. 13 November 2026

    Hardware encoders and a Mac Settings window

    Admins see which encoders the server can really use, notifications check for news while Arcfin is open, and the Mac gets a Settings window of its own.

  3. 12 November 2026

    Admin tools and notifications

    An Admin tab with server settings and a metadata editor, notifications for new episodes and films, and a long press on your picture to switch profile.

  4. 12 November 2026

    Home and library extras

    Collections your admin features, unwatched counts on posters, tags, and a choice of episode or show artwork.

  5. 12 November 2026

    Downloads that wait for space

    Downloads wait instead of failing when the device is full, Manage Storage lists the largest first, and Seerr shows a movie’s collection.

  6. 11 November 2026

    One-tap Seerr

    Connect Seerr with Quick Connect, and approve or decline requests right in the app.

  7. 11 November 2026

    Live TV

    Channels, a guide, recordings and radio, with pause and rewind, reminders, and favourite channels on Home and the Top Shelf.

  8. 9 November 2026

    0.4.0: each platform on its own

    iPhone and iPad, Mac and Apple TV each run on screens of their own, so a change on one can’t break another.

  9. 9 November 2026

    Ready for App Review

    Complete privacy details, full credits and a license that allows the App Store, clearer messages when a video stops, and Quick Connect next to your profile.

  10. 9 November 2026

    Apple TV, faster and steadier

    Title pages no longer hold the processor at full load, Home moves one row at a time, and sheets open in a wide panel that fits them.

  11. 9 November 2026

    A fuller Discover

    Big cards mixed with rows of what’s trending, titles like your last watch, what’s popular on your streaming services, genres and what’s coming soon.

  12. 8 November 2026

    Your Profile on every platform

    See your account, libraries, parental controls and permissions in Settings, and how many titles each library holds.

  13. 8 November 2026

    Spotlight Home on Apple TV

    Home opens on the featured title’s trailer and moves through the rows one at a time, and title pages show when a title would end.

  14. 7 November 2026

    A richer Top Shelf

    What you’re watching, with logos and progress, plus Next Up, in the Apple TV’s top row.

  15. 2 November 2026

    One Live Activity for Smart Downloads

    A Smart Downloads run shows as a single Live Activity, and Home’s hero fills an iPhone in landscape.

October 2026

  1. 31 October 2026

    A new coral look

    A coral colour and a new app icon, in light and dark appearance.

  2. 30 October 2026

    Each platform on its own views

    iPhone and iPad, Mac and Apple TV each get their own screens over shared logic, so a change on one can’t break another.

  3. 29 October 2026

    0.3.0: polish

    Skips in downloads, a letter picker for big libraries, Compatibility Mode, a welcome tour, and the Apple TV hero trailer in full. Screenshots now come straight from each release.

  4. 29 October 2026

    Offline skips and “In Library” everywhere

    Downloads skip the recap, intro, credits and preview just like streaming, and Discover, Search and Seerr pages recognise titles you already have.

  5. 29 October 2026

    A welcome tour and What’s New

    New installs get a short tour of Arcfin, and everyone gets a card with the news once after each update.

  6. 29 October 2026

    Trailers on the Apple TV Home screen

    The featured title’s trailer plays in full with the details out of the way, and Settings gets a full-screen layout with a working Back.

  7. 28 October 2026

    Help for videos that won’t play

    Compatibility Mode and switches that send HDR and Dolby Vision through the server, plus a picture again for Blu-ray HEVC on the Aether engine.

  8. 28 October 2026

    A to Z through big libraries

    A letter index jumps straight to a letter in a library sorted by name, with a row of letters on Apple TV.

  9. 27 October 2026

    A new website

    This site, rebuilt in eleven languages, with a changelog that keeps itself up to date.

  10. 26 October 2026

    Apple TV, tidied up

    Free scrubbing on the Siri Remote’s touch surface, even spacing on title pages, a steadier Home screen and a cleaner Stats screen.

  11. 26 October 2026

    Signed out? Sign straight back in

    Arcfin notices when a server stops accepting your sign-in and asks you to sign in again, and title pages update as soon as you stop watching.

  12. 26 October 2026

    0.2.0: eleven languages

    Arcfin speaks ten more languages, Home updates live, and Report a Problem and Share Diagnostics arrive.

  13. 25 October 2026

    0.1.2 and checks on every change

    Live Active Sessions and a reworked player, and every pull request is now built and tested on all three platforms.

  14. 22 October 2026

    0.1.1: part of the system

    Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, Spotlight, Handoff, SharePlay, a light appearance, new Home shelves and the Apple TV screensaver.

  15. 20 October 2026

    Accessibility and a Cast dashboard

    VoiceOver grouping, Dynamic Type everywhere, a Cast dashboard, a sleep timer and Home Screen quick actions.

  16. 4 October 2026

    Aether becomes the default engine

    FFmpeg demuxing with hardware decoding plays almost anything, with AVFoundation and VLC as fallbacks.

September 2026

  1. 24 September 2026

    Arcfin Sync

    Settings sync across devices through the Arcfin Sync server plugin, and admins can lock the ones that matter.

  2. 22 September 2026

    Apple TV on TestFlight

    The first Apple TV build, with a focus-driven layout of its own.

  3. 21 September 2026

    Open source

    The code is published under the AGPL-3.0, and the project opens to contributors.

  4. 20 September 2026

    First TestFlight build

    Arcfin 0.1.0 reaches testers on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  5. 19 September 2026

    Casting and watching together

    Chromecast, Jellyfin’s SyncPlay, Smart Downloads and Active Sessions for server admins.

  6. 18 September 2026

    Real playback and downloads

    The player moves to Jellyfin’s playback API with VLC as a second engine, and downloads arrive with Live Activities.

May 2026

  1. 4 May 2026

    Seerr

    Browse and request titles through Seerr, beside your own library.

  2. 3 May 2026

    Mac and Apple TV from the start

    macOS and tvOS join iPhone and iPad in the first week, with a layout for the Siri Remote and a Top Shelf extension.

  3. 3 May 2026

    First commit

    A Swift package for each part of the app, the design system, and the first screens: Home, Library, Search and title pages.
