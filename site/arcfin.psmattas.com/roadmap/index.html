Roadmap

From first commit to the App Store.

On this page

What’s next

  1. 1.0.0

    The App Store

    Live TV with channels, a guide and recordings, plus server admin tools. Then Arcfin goes to Apple for App Store review.

  2. 0.4.0

    Platform split

    Each platform gets its own views over shared logic, so a change on one can’t break another. Nothing changes on screen.

  3. 0.3.0

    Polish

    Skipping intros and credits offline, “In Library” everywhere, a welcome tour and a round of Apple TV fixes.

Plans can change. The milestones show what’s in each version as it happens.

Now

Version 0.2.0 on TestFlight

All four platforms with near feature parity: playback, downloads, Stats, Seerr and eleven languages.

Join the TestFlight betaRead the changelog

October 2026

  1. 27 October 2026

    A new website

    This site, rebuilt in eleven languages, with a changelog that keeps itself up to date.

  2. 26 October 2026

    0.2.0: eleven languages

    Arcfin speaks ten more languages, Home updates live, and Report a Problem and Share Diagnostics arrive.

  3. 25 October 2026

    0.1.2 and checks on every change

    Live Active Sessions and a reworked player, and every pull request is now built and tested on all three platforms.

  4. 22 October 2026

    0.1.1: part of the system

    Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, Spotlight, Handoff, SharePlay, a light appearance, new Home shelves and the Apple TV screensaver.

  5. 20 October 2026

    Accessibility and a Cast dashboard

    VoiceOver grouping, Dynamic Type everywhere, a Cast dashboard, a sleep timer and Home Screen quick actions.

  6. 4 October 2026

    Aether becomes the default engine

    FFmpeg demuxing with hardware decoding plays almost anything, with AVFoundation and VLC as fallbacks.

September 2026

  1. 24 September 2026

    Arcfin Sync

    Settings sync across devices through the Arcfin Sync server plugin, and admins can lock the ones that matter.

  2. 22 September 2026

    Apple TV on TestFlight

    The first Apple TV build, with a focus-driven layout of its own.

  3. 21 September 2026

    Open source

    The code is published under the AGPL-3.0, and the project opens to contributors.

  4. 20 September 2026

    First TestFlight build

    Arcfin 0.1.0 reaches testers on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  5. 19 September 2026

    Casting and watching together

    Chromecast, Jellyfin’s SyncPlay, Smart Downloads and Active Sessions for server admins.

  6. 18 September 2026

    Real playback and downloads

    The player moves to Jellyfin’s playback API with VLC as a second engine, and downloads arrive with Live Activities.

May 2026

  1. 4 May 2026

    Seerr

    Browse and request titles through Seerr, beside your own library.

  2. 3 May 2026

    Mac and Apple TV from the start

    macOS and tvOS join iPhone and iPad in the first week, with a layout for the Siri Remote and a Top Shelf extension.

  3. 3 May 2026

    First commit

    A Swift package for each part of the app, the design system, and the first screens: Home, Library, Search and title pages.
