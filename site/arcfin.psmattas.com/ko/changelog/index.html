  • A Cast mini controller above the tab bar. (#141, #144)
  • Download queue controls: pause the queue, download next, cancel all. (#142)
  • The profile's Jellyfin parental controls and permissions, custom request headers for servers behind an access gateway, and offline mode when the server can't be reached.
  • Light appearance on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • iPad and Mac open the player in its own window.

개선

  • A new launch animation, a redesigned collection page, and the server and sign-in screens reworked to match the app.

  • Title pages show Resume after leaving the player. (#149)

제거

  • Streamystats; Stats now come from Jellyfin.
