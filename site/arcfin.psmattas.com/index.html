Your Jellyfin library, beautifully played.

Arcfin is an open-source Jellyfin client for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch, with CarPlay in the car. It plays almost anything your server has, downloads for watching offline, and feels at home on every screen you own.

Features

Everything on your server, wherever you watch or listen.

Arcfin talks straight to the Jellyfin server you already run. Here is what it does with it.

Watching

Plays almost anything your server has.

Arcfin picks the best of three playback engines for each file, so HEVC, HDR, surround sound and odd old containers play without fuss.

  • Three engines. Aether (FFmpeg demuxing with hardware decoding) by default, with AVFoundation and VLC as fallbacks when a file won’t open.

  • Skip what you’ve seen. Skip Intro, Recap, Credits and Preview, plus chapters, scrubbing previews and Up Next with a countdown.

  • In the player. Playback speed, Previous and Next, Cast & Characters, “Are you still watching?” and a sleep timer, with surround and Atmos audio.

  • Your subtitles, your way. Text and image subtitles with your own styling, and audio or subtitle switching without reloading.

  • Beyond the screen. AirPlay, Chromecast, Picture in Picture, SharePlay over FaceTime and Jellyfin’s SyncPlay.

  • Play On and remote control. Send what you’re watching to another device signed in to your server, and control it from here, from the Jellyfin web app or from your Apple Watch.

Music

Your music, as well as your films.

Arcfin plays the music on your Jellyfin server too, with a Now Playing screen built around the cover.

  • The whole library. Artists with an A–Z bar, albums, songs, genres with covers, playlists and favourites, each with its own search, plus Made for You playlists. Artist pages show the artist’s logo and backdrop when your server has them.

  • Now Playing. The cover up front, synced and plain lyrics, a visualizer, a quality badge, the queue and what played earlier, Repeat, Shuffle and a sleep timer.

  • Offline. Download songs, albums, playlists or everything by an artist, with smart downloads, a storage limit and a Live Activity.

  • Everywhere you listen. SharePlay, CarPlay with voice search, Siri, interactive widgets in every size, AirPlay and casting.

  • Your listening. ListenBrainz scrobbling, likes and dislikes, and importing your listening history from other music services.

  • What’s Playing? and Year in Music. Recognise a song that’s playing around you and find it, and look back on your year of listening.

Browsing

A Home screen that knows what’s next.

Continue Watching, Next Up, Recently Added, recommendations and collections, in the order you choose. What you watch on other devices shows up while the app is open.

  • Title pages with depth. Cast, review scores, age ratings, where to watch, trailers, More Like This, and the collections and playlists a title is in.

  • A library you can dig through. Filters, sorting, genres, playlists, collections, Hide Watched and Surprise Me.

  • Search inside libraries. Movies, Shows, Collections and Playlists each have a search of their own.

  • Search everything. Your server and Discover in one search, with optional history.

  • Spoiler Protection. Hides episode thumbnails and descriptions until you’ve watched them.

Downloads

Take your library offline.

Download films and episodes on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your progress syncs back to the server the next time you’re online.

  • Fast. Large files download in parallel ranges, several titles at once, and keep going in the background.

  • Live Activity. Progress and time left on the Lock Screen, including when a download is paused or waiting for Wi-Fi.

  • Smart Downloads. Keeps the next episodes of your shows ready and removes watched ones, within a storage budget you set. Keep Downloaded does the same for one show from its page.

  • In your control. Quality, mobile data, pausing in Low Power and Low Data Mode, and automatic clean-up.

  • The full player. Downloads, and video files you open with Browse Files, play in the same player as streaming, with chapters, scrubbing previews and Up Next.

Profiles

Everyone gets their own Arcfin.

Each person gets their own profile, with their own recommendations and progress, behind its own lock.

  • Locked when you want. Face ID, Touch ID or a PIN for each profile, plus the server’s parental controls and access schedule.

  • Set Up Nearby Device. Sign in an Apple TV, iPad or Mac from your iPhone or iPad, with every profile you pick and their Seerr and ListenBrainz, over your own network. Both screens show a code to compare, nothing is sent until you allow it on the new device, and passwords are never sent.

  • Devices & Sync. With the Arcfin Sync server plugin, your settings follow you to your other devices: automatically, after asking first, or not at all. You can also send one device’s settings to all the others.

  • An Admin tab for server admins. The server dashboard, scheduled tasks and activity, libraries, users, devices, plugins, API keys, logs, backups, server settings and a title’s metadata editor. Active Sessions shows who’s watching and why a stream transcodes, and lets you pause, stop or message them.

  • Arcfin for Everyone. Admins set defaults and locks for every Arcfin app on the server, or for one person.

Across your devices

Part of the system, not just an app.

Arcfin works with Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and the Lock Screen, so the next episode is never more than a tap or a sentence away.

  • Siri and Shortcuts. Play a title, play a random movie, download the next episode of a show, or open Discover.

  • Widgets and controls. Continue Watching and Recently Added widgets, Control Center controls, Spotlight and Handoff.

  • New episode alerts. Notifications when new episodes arrive, with Quiet Hours.

  • On the big screen. On Apple TV, Top Shelf with trailers from your server, Spotlight Home, Always Ask Who’s Watching and a screensaver made from your own artwork. On the Mac, every player control in the menu bar.

  • In the car. CarPlay, on iPhone, lays out your music like the system’s music app, with Home, New, Radio and Library, rows of covers, A–Z artists and album pages. Search has its own tab, with voice search and quick buttons, and Now Playing shows whether Shuffle and Repeat are on. It starts signed in from the car, keeps your profile’s PIN, and has a widget on the CarPlay dashboard.

  • On your wrist. The Apple Watch app plays your music, with playlists kept on the watch to play without your phone. It’s also a remote for Arcfin on another device, with the Digital Crown for volume.

And more

The details add up.

Stats

An optional tab with All, Movies & Shows and Music, by week, month, year or all time: time watched, streaks, your top shows, songs and artists, and the genres you come back to, with a card to share in Story or Post size.

Live TV

Channels and the guide, narrowed to movies, series, news, kids or sports, with radio and search. Pause and rewind a channel, switch from a channel list over the picture, record a programme or a whole series, and set reminders. Favourite, on-now and recent channels sit on Home.

Seerr requests

Browse, request and keep a watchlist through your own Seerr server, signed in per profile.

Optional

TMDB extras

Add your own TMDB key for review scores, where to watch and what’s popular.

Optional

ListenBrainz

Add your own ListenBrainz token to a profile. Arcfin sends your listens and loves to it, and Stats and Year in Music can count from it.

On a Jellyfin 12.4 server, Jellyfin’s own ListenBrainz similarity provider also feeds Similar Artists.

Optional

Player in its own window

On iPad and Mac, keep the player in its own window and keep browsing beside it.

Eleven languages

English, German, French, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Italian, Korean, Dutch and Russian, with a language picker in Settings.

Accessible

VoiceOver labels throughout, Dynamic Type, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion.

Help built in

Report a Problem and Share Diagnostics in Settings. Diagnostics stay on your device until you choose to send them.

Light and dark

Follows your system appearance on iPhone, iPad and Mac. The player and Apple TV always stay dark.

Arcfin Sync plugin

A companion plugin for your Jellyfin server: Devices & Sync, so your settings follow you, defaults and locks your admin sets for everyone, and smaller downloads.

See every screen

A few things can’t work everywhere: Chromecast is iPhone and iPad only, because Google’s SDK is. CarPlay is iPhone only, and plays music. Live Activities and Lock Screen widgets are iPhone only. Apple TV has no downloads (it has no lasting storage) and no Face ID or Touch ID, and its screensaver runs inside Arcfin, because Apple TV doesn’t let apps provide a system screensaver.

Privacy

No Arcfin server in the middle.

Arcfin connects your device straight to your own server. There’s no account with us, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting service. Passwords, tokens and keys stay in your device’s Keychain.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need my own server?

Yes. Arcfin is a client for Jellyfin, a free media server you run yourself. You need a Jellyfin server of your own, or an account on one that someone you trust runs. Arcfin doesn’t provide, host or link to any content.

Is Arcfin on the App Store?

Not yet. Arcfin is in public beta on TestFlight. It goes to Apple for App Store review at version 0.5.1.

Install TestFlight from the App Store, open the invite on your iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple TV, and new builds arrive automatically. The Apple Watch app and CarPlay come with the iPhone app.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26, Macs with macOS 26, and Apple TV with tvOS 26. The Mac version is a real Mac app with its own interface, and Apple TV has its own layout built for the Siri Remote.

The Apple Watch app, for watchOS 26, plays your music and works as a remote. It comes with the iPhone app, and so does CarPlay, for your music in the car.

Do I need to install anything on my server?

No. Arcfin works with any Jellyfin server as it is. There’s an optional companion plugin, Arcfin Sync, that your server’s admin can install so your settings follow you between devices and downloads get smaller.

Is Arcfin an official Jellyfin app?

No. Arcfin is an independent, third-party client. It isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project, so please don’t ask the Jellyfin team for help with it. Use the support page instead.

Does Arcfin collect my data?

No. There’s no Arcfin server, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting service. The app talks directly to the servers you add. The Privacy Policy lists every outside service and when it’s used.

Something doesn’t work. What should I do?

Use Settings › About › Report a Problem in the app, or open an issue. Include your device, OS version and Jellyfin server version. More help is on the support page.
