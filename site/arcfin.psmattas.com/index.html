Everything you need to enjoy your library

A native Jellyfin experience built around the way you watch, browse, and manage your own media.

Home

Continue Watching, Next Up, Recently Added, and recommended shelves put the right thing in reach.

Library

Browse, filter, search, and sort your library, including sorting by genre.

Player

Direct play or transcode with automatic quality stepping, chapters, playback speed, custom subtitle styling, and Up Next.

Playback everywhere

AirPlay, Chromecast, Picture in Picture, Now Playing, Control Center, SyncPlay, and skip intro, recap, or credits.

Downloads

Offline playback with Wi-Fi-only and quality controls, plus a Live Activity for download progress.

Trailers

Play local trailers, with an in-app YouTube fallback when a title has no trailer on your server.

Requests & stats

Browse and request content through Seerr, and view your watch data through the Streamystats dashboard.

Profiles

Switch profiles quickly and protect each profile with Face ID or a PIN app lock.

Settings

A grouped hub for Playback, Downloads, Appearance, Notifications, Server, Integrations, and policy links.

Your server, your rules

Connects straight to a Jellyfin server you already run. No account with us, because there is no us in the middle.

Home

Help shape Arcfin

Arcfin is open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Report bugs, request features, or open a pull request — sign in with your existing GitHub or GitLab account, no separate account needed.

Contribution details View Source Organization
