Arcfin

A premium native Jellyfin client for iPhone and iPad. Your own media server, a fast, polished native app.

Built for Liquid Glass

A ground-up SwiftUI app for iOS 26 and iPadOS 26 — not a wrapped web view, not a port.

Your server, your rules

Connects straight to a Jellyfin server you already run. No account with us, because there is no us in the middle.

AirPlay, Chromecast & PiP

Cast to a TV, AirPlay audio and video, or keep watching in Picture in Picture while you do something else.

Downloads for offline

Take a library with you — download episodes and movies for offline playback, Wi-Fi-only if you want.

Per-profile lock

Face ID or a PIN per profile, with a Netflix-style profile picker on launch.

Seerr & Streamystats

Request new content and browse your own watch stats, right alongside your library.

Arcfin talks directly to your Jellyfin server (and, if you connect them, your own Seerr and Streamystats instances) from your device. There's no server of ours in between, and no account required to use the app.
