Legal

Data Protection

The details data protection laws such as the UK and EU GDPR and the CCPA ask for: who the controller is, the legal bases, and your rights.

Effective 16 November 2026

On this page

In short: Arcfin has no backend. Personal data either stays on your device or goes straight from your device to a server you run or chose to connect. We aren’t the controller of that server’s data, and we hold essentially nothing about you.

This page adds to the Privacy Policy.

Data controller

For the small amount of data that relates to the app itself (not your Jellyfin server’s own data), the controller is:

Puranjay Savar Mattas
Contact: privacy.arcfin@psmattas.com

We haven’t appointed a Data Protection Officer, as one isn’t required for processing of this scope.

We don’t process any data for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making.

Your Jellyfin server’s data

Your watch history, library, user accounts and similar data live on your Jellyfin server (and Seerr, if connected), which you or someone you trust controls. We aren’t the controller or processor of that data. Requests to access, correct or delete it go to whoever runs that server, which may be you.

International transfers

Because Arcfin has no server, none of your data is transferred to us. Data goes directly from your device to the services you set up, wherever they’re hosted.

Retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete it, sign out or delete the app. We hold nothing on a server, so there’s nothing for us to retain.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port data about you, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. In practice:

  • Access, deletion and portability: the data is on your device, and deleting the app deletes it.
  • Withdrawing consent: turn off Local Network, Location, Camera or other permissions in your device’s settings at any time. The features that need them will stop working.
  • Anything else: email privacy.arcfin@psmattas.com and we’ll reply within 30 days.

In the UK and EU you can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that’s the Information Commissioner’s Office.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

We don’t sell or share personal information, and haven’t in the past 12 months, because we don’t collect any. Since we hold no personal information on a server, there’s nothing further to disclose in response to an access request.

Security

Sign-ins are stored in the system Keychain, encrypted by the operating system. Requests to your server use the transport your server is set up for. We strongly recommend HTTPS for any server reachable from outside your home.

Setting up an Apple TV from an iPhone or iPad agrees fresh keys on each try and shows a code from them on both screens, so a device in between can’t take part unnoticed. Nothing is sent until you’ve allowed it on the Apple TV and confirmed the code on your iPhone or iPad, and everything after that is encrypted.
